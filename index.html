Lotus Lens.

An open-source MTG Arena draft copilot. Draft, build, play and debrief, on your own machine.

by Hidden Lotus Tech

Install Soon What it does

What it does

A quiet second opinion for every Limited event: from P1P1 to the last game.

Draft

Ranks each pack as you see it, tracks your colors and signals, and shows what's likely to wheel.

Build

Checks your 40: curve, creature count, removal, and a mana-base audit so your splash actually casts.

Play

A calm in-match view of draw odds, outs and the threats that matter. Off by default in tournament mode.

Debrief

Turn-by-turn replays and a short postmortem after each match and event, so you learn from the run.

Local-first

Runs on your PC. Your logs and decks stay there. No account needed.

Read-only

It only reads the Player.log file Arena already writes. It never touches the game, injects code or clicks for you.

Bring your own AI

Connect Claude, Cursor or a local model (Ollama) through MCP, and ask about your draft in plain words.

In the glass

Real panes from a live Limited run — pilot brief and career form, side by side.

FAQ

Is it allowed? Will it get me banned?

Lotus Lens only reads the log file Arena writes, the same approach long-standing community trackers use. It never modifies, injects into or automates the game. It's unofficial and not endorsed by Wizards. Use it at your own risk.

Can I use live advice in tournaments?

Don't use live in-match advice in Qualifiers or the Arena Championship. Tournament mode turns live advice off and keeps prep and review on. Lotus Lens suggests it when it sees those events.

What data does it read, and where does it go?

It reads your local Player.log and keeps everything on your machine. There's no telemetry. Bring-your-own-AI sends only what your own assistant asks for. The optional hosted coach, when it exists, gets a minimal game summary only: never your full log, account tokens or opponent names.

Do I need to pay or make an account?

No. The free version is the full app, with no account.

Which platforms?

Windows first (live Arena). macOS is experimental. Linux works for analysis and MCP.

Where do the card ratings come from?

Ratings come from 17Lands' public data, credited below, and appear for a new set from its twelfth day on Arena. Card data comes from Scryfall.
